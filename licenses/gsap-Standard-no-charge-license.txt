connect-ai-slides-web does not contain GSAP source code. `npm install` downloads the
gsap package from npm. When a story uses a gsap/* or hyperframes/* block, `build`
copies gsap.min.js and the plugins that block needs, unchanged and with their
licence header, into your own out/<stage>/assets/vendor/ folder. Keep that header.
